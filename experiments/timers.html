LOCAL INTERFACE STUDYPicking playground

One ring, three rhythms.

One set of ID-addressed state, two rendering interfaces. Click a small timer to switch. Choose what clicking a step does, or Shift-click to select. Drag the centre to scrub its phase.

Probability adds an arc; marking adds a small diamond. The playhead sends commands through the same handler as pointer input.

This is a proof of concept, excluded from the production demo build.
