Candidate verification experiment — throwaway

Does a cached colour lookup followed by an isolated 1×1 redraw prevent unrelated-object picks, while preserving thin targets? Both reads require alpha 255. A returned shape must have fully opaque coverage at the sampled pixel; draw order may be ambiguous at overlapping edges.

Reference coverage comes from independently rendering each shape into a full-size mask. The primary check rejects returned IDs with zero reference coverage. Stricter checks also look for exact agreement about opaque pixels; failures there expose rasterisation differences. The library is unchanged.
